Hypothesis

Helping users identify their primary symptom before browsing products will increase engagement with relevant categories and improve downstream conversion.

What changes

Instead of immediately showing the existing 10% email popup, users first complete a short symptom discovery step. After selecting their primary symptom, they are redirected to the most relevant category page. The existing 10% email popup remains unchanged and appears later in the journey.
