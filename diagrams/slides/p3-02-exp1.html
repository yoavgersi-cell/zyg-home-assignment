Hypothesis

Helping users identify their primary symptom before browsing products will increase engagement with relevant categories and improve downstream conversion.

What changes

The journey - not the popup. Users first complete a short symptom discovery step, then land on the most relevant category page. The existing 10% email popup remains unchanged, appearing later in the journey.
