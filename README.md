## Part 1 — Market & competitive landscape

**Category / vertical:** Menopause & perimenopause wellness — a lifestage vertical inside women's health / DTC beauty & supplements. Stripes is a hormone-free, clinically-backed, celebrity-founded brand spanning "scalp to vag." *The Inside Addition* lives in the ingestible / daily-symptom-support segment — the most paid-social-driven corner of the category.

Cold buyers are served ads by **three rings of competition** at once:

| Ring | Players | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct — menopause DTC** | Womaness, Kindra (Bayer), Bonafide (Relizen/Ristela), State Of, Pause Well-Aging, Wile | Closest substitutes |
| **Rx / telehealth** | Midi Health, Alloy, Evernow, Winona | The "treat it medically / HRT" lane; quiz funnels |
| **Adjacent paid-social machines** | **Happy Mammoth (Hormone Harmony)**, O Positiv (MENO/URO), Nutrafol, Ritual, Perelel, Arrae | The playbooks actually winning the feed |

**The one to beat:** Happy Mammoth / Hormone Harmony — the category's #1 paid spender — scaled on relentless symptom-led, quasi-clinical UGC and advertorials, but carries a credibility/trust tax. That gap is Stripes' opening.

**What's already running (5 angles):** symptom shock-hook · fear/hormone-panic · doctor/clinical authority · raw UGC testimonial · advertorial listicle.
**The open lane (the 6th):** credible, empowered symptom relief — *a real face you trust + real science + zero hormone anxiety.* Stripes' unfair advantages to fill it: **Naomi Watts** (a face no one can copy), **hormone-free** (de-risks the #1 objection), and **clinical + premium brand equity.**

## Part 2 — Hero product launch

### 1. Creative angle
> **"The daily that meets menopause where it actually hits."**
> *"You were told to just push through the 2 a.m. wake-ups, the fog, the mood swings. From Naomi Watts — one hormone-free daily, made to give you your nights, your focus and your calm back."*

- **Why:** symptom-specific (sleep→mood→focus cascade) stops the scroll where education can't; leads with Naomi + hormone-free + clinical; empowered, permission-giving tone vs. the shame/panic in the feed.
- **For whom:** women 45–60 in peri/menopause, symptomatic, HRT-hesitant or -excluded, burned by a generic supplement that did nothing.
- **Why it wins:** beats Happy Mammoth on credibility (not spend — a defensible moat), beats clinical brands on emotional resonance, and hormone-free neutralizes the category's biggest objection.

### 2. Landing page
**Page type: a dedicated long-form landing page**, not the catalog PDP — cold traffic needs education, proof and objection-handling in one branded, testable scroll. (Tradeoff: costs more than a PDP tweak; justified as the highest-leverage cold-CVR surface and the reusable template for every future angle test.)

**Layout (10 sections, each earning its place):** ① Hero → ② Problem agitation → ③ Founder turn (Naomi) → ④ How it works / science → ⑤ What's inside → ⑥ Social proof → ⑦ Why Stripes vs. alternatives → ⑧ Offer → ⑨ FAQ → ⑩ Final CTA + risk reversal. Sticky mobile Add-to-Cart throughout. *Full copy for every section is in the deck (slides 11–12).*

**Visual guidelines:** warm/credible palette (berry, coral CTA, gold, cream, ink); real women 45–60 in natural light; warm, in-on-it, never fear-mongering voice; editorial whitespace, mobile-first, one coral CTA color.

### 3. Funnel wireframe
Ad → Landing page → Cart/checkout → **One-click upsell** → Thank-you page → Onboarding flow → Replenish & retain → Advocate & cross-sell. **The first order buys the customer; post-purchase (subscription, retention, cross-sell across the "scalp to vag" range) is where the model makes money.**

### 4. Measuring success
**North star: LTV : CAC ≥ 3:1, CAC payback < 3 months.** Connected tree:
- **Acquisition:** Ad CTR ≥ 1.5%, efficient CPC/CPM, falling cost-per-LP-visit
- **Conversion:** LP CVR (cold) 3–5%, AOV ↑ via upsell, subscribe take-rate ≥ 30%, upsell take-rate 10–15%
- **Retention / LTV:** m3 subscription retention ≥ 60%, 60/90-day repeat growing, refund rate < 8%, 90-day LTV > CAC × 1.5

*How they connect:* CTR feeds LP visits → CVR × AOV sets first-order revenue → subscribe & upsell set day-1 LTV → retention compounds it → LTV vs. CAC decides whether we pour fuel on the fire. Targets are launch hypotheses to beat.

## Part 3 — First A/B tests (ranked by leverage)

1. **PDP hero: symptom-led value prop + proof above the fold** (top of funnel). *Hypothesis:* the live PDP opens on product, not problem; leading with the symptom→relief promise + rating + hormone-free badge lifts add-to-cart from cold traffic. First because it's the highest-traffic surface and validates the launch angle on owned traffic before big spend.
2. **Subscription-first offer presentation** (offer step). *Hypothesis:* defaulting to Subscribe-&-Save with clear savings + cancel-anytime raises subscription take-rate without hurting CVR — moves the retention half of the north star. Cheap to ship.
3. **Post-purchase one-click upsell** (post-purchase). *Hypothesis:* a one-click upsell on the thank-you step lifts AOV with zero added CAC and no checkout-funnel risk — the safest revenue to add while 1–2 run.

## Part 4 — Agentic approach

**Automate (agent-led):** competitive research (ad-library scraping, angle mining), A/B variant generation + traffic allocation + significance calls + readouts.
**Agent + human:** angle generation, landing-page assembly, funnel/offer logic.
**Human-led (non-negotiable):** medical / ad-claim compliance, and brand voice & founder authenticity.
*Rule: agents compress the 80% that's pattern-work; humans keep the 20% that carries brand and legal risk.*

**The one I'd build — "The Angle-to-Test Loop"** (creative-intelligence & LP testing agent):
- **Monitors:** category ad libraries (new creative/angles/longevity as a spend proxy), own ad + LP analytics (CTR, CVR, AOV, scroll/drop-off), and voice-of-customer (reviews, tickets, on-site search).
- **Decides:** which angles are fatiguing, which fresh angle/section to test next (ranked by expected lift), and when a live test has hit significance.
- **Triggers:** generates a new LP variant from a modular template, routes every health claim to a human/regulatory gate, launches the A/B test, reallocates budget to winners, posts a plain-language readout.
- **How:** data pipeline → LLM clusters angles & drafts copy against the template → guardrails (brand-voice checks + hard human claim gate) → experimentation API launches/reads the test → human approves anything customer-facing. The human moves from *doing* the work to *editing and approving* it.

- **All health claims** pass a human/regulatory review before any ad or page goes live.
