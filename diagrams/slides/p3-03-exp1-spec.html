Hypothesis

Discover first, subscribe later

New visitors are pushed to subscribe before they know which product fits. Routing them to the most relevant category first should cut decision friction and lift progression into the shopping journey.

Primary KPI
Product Detail Page view rate
Share of popup viewers who reach a PDP. This is the progression the test is built to move.
Supporting KPIs
Subscription conversion rate
Revenue per visitor
Email signup rate

Guardrails - discovery should lift the journey without costing us email capture or revenue.

Why this test first

It sits at the top of the funnel, where the most traffic is lost, and reuses the site's existing category structure - high potential impact for low dev effort. The natural first move.

UX refinements (hypothesis unchanged)
  • Labels mirror the site's category names so the handoff feels native.
  • Icons + chevrons so options read as navigation, not a form.
  • Slimmer hero to elevate the question and the tap targets.
  • "General support" as a no-wrong-answer path for the unsure.
