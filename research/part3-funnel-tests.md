# Part 3 - Funnel Optimization

I looked at the live site (the PDP, the cart drawer, and checkout). Three tests, in the order I'd run them.

## Test 1 - Make the PDP hero about her, not the bottle
**What's there now:** the hero leads with the product name and a technical line ("Daily Menopause Symptom Support Probiotic Supplement + Vitamin Blend"). The symptoms it actually helps with - stress, hot flashes, brain fog - are hidden in a collapsed "Details" accordion. A cold visitor from a paid ad lands with no emotional hook.
**Hypothesis:** leading with a symptom-led promise (plus the star rating) above the fold will lift add-to-cart from cold traffic.
**Change:** swap the technical subhead for a symptom line ("For the hot flashes, brain fog and stress no one warned you about - one hormone-free daily"), pull the top 3 symptoms and the rating/review count above the fold, keep one clear CTA.
**Where in the funnel:** the PDP / landing page (top of funnel).
**Why first:** highest-traffic surface, biggest CVR lever, and it keeps the promise the ad made (message match). Everything downstream depends on winning here.

## Test 2 - Clean, on-product social proof, higher up
**What's there now:** the rating shows ~4.5 stars, but reviews sit far down the page and the feed is mixed with reviews for other products (a body oil, a travel size). For a skeptical buyer that's both buried and diluted.
**Hypothesis:** showing a review count next to the stars and 2-3 curated, supplement-specific reviews near the top - and filtering the feed to this product - will raise trust and CVR.
**Change:** add the review count above the fold, insert a short proof block high on the page, and scope the review feed to The Inside Addition.
**Where in the funnel:** PDP, upper-mid (the decision point).
**Why second:** trust is this audience's main objection ("I've tried things that didn't work"), and right now the proof is hard to find and off-topic. Cheap to fix, direct CVR impact.

## Test 3 - Add a one-click post-purchase upsell
**What's there now:** there's a good cart cross-sell ("Spend more, Save more"), but nothing after checkout - the flow goes straight to the Shopify confirmation. That's AOV left on the table.
**Hypothesis:** a one-click upsell on the confirmation step (a topical like Vag of Honor, or a second bottle) will lift AOV with zero added CAC.
**Change:** insert a one-tap post-purchase upsell between payment and confirmation.
**Where in the funnel:** post-purchase.
**Why third:** pure-margin AOV with no risk to the core conversion funnel - the safest revenue to add while Tests 1 and 2 run.

*Tests map to the KPIs: Tests 1-2 move conversion rate; Test 3 moves AOV (and LTV).*
