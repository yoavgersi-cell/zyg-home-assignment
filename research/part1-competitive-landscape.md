# Part 1 — Competitors (final write-up)

## How we define a competitor
A competitor is a brand the **same woman** might buy to solve the **same problem** (feeling better in peri/menopause). Two questions, both must be yes:
1. **Same woman?** Aimed at a woman in perimenopause or menopause.
2. **Same need?** She's buying it to ease symptoms and feel better.

Then one question sorts it (you can check it on their homepage):
3. **Is menopause the brand's whole identity?** Yes = **Direct.** No, a broader brand that catches her as one of several audiences = **Indirect** (these often fight hardest for her on paid).

---

## Direct competitors (menopause IS the brand)
1. **Womaness** — broad menopause line (Unilever-backed). Ads lead on vaginal dryness + skincare.
2. **Kindra** — menopause essentials (P&G-backed). Doctor/expert-led ads, intimate care.
3. **Bonafide (Relizen)** — hormone-free menopause. The one direct brand actually buying menopause search ("symptoms of menopause", "vaginal atrophy").
4. **Estroven** — drugstore menopause supplement. Barely advertises (retail-led).
5. **Wile** — founder-led perimenopause DTC. Currently dark on paid (0 active ads).
6. **Pause Well-Aging** — menopause skincare. Small paid player.
7. **Valerie** — daily perimenopause supplement. The closest product-for-product rival to The Inside Addition.

## Indirect competitors (broader brand, wins her through paid)
1. **Happy Mammoth** — hormone/gut brand. The category's paid monster (~660 active FB/IG ads) + growing branded paid search. Not a menopause brand.
2. **Midi Health** — menopause telehealth/HRT. The paid-**search** giant (~155K visits/mo, climbing; bids on rivals).
3. **O Positiv** — women's-wellness brand (URO). Most diverse social mix incl. TikTok. Menopause (MENO) is a small sub-line.
4. **Nutrafol** — hair thinning (45+, GLP-1 angle). Big paid, Unilever-owned.
5. **Ritual** — women's multivitamins. Big paid, but prenatal-focused — weak menopause overlap.
6. **Evernow** — menopause telehealth/HRT (a smaller Midi).
7. **Foria** — CBD sexual wellness; reaches menopause via intimacy/vaginal comfort.
8. **Better Not Younger** — hair/scalp care for women 40+.

*Also on the radar (address-and-move-on): Alloy, Winona (telehealth/HRT); State Of Menopause; The Better Menopause (UK).*

## What it means for Stripes (the read)
1. **The paid leaders are all INDIRECT, broad brands** — Happy Mammoth (FB/IG), Midi (search), O Positiv (multi-platform). The real menopause brands barely spend.
2. **Almost none of that paid muscle is actually about menopause** — it's hormones/gut (Happy Mammoth), HRT (Midi), vaginal probiotics (O Positiv), prenatal (Ritual), hair (Nutrafol).
3. **Stripes is a small paid player (~38 ads) — and isn't even advertising the supplement** (its ads are skincare/body). So The Inside Addition launch is genuinely open ground.
4. **The wedge:** nobody credibly owns "a real face you trust + real science + hormone-free" on paid. Stripes has the assets already in hand — **Naomi Watts (founder)** in creative and a live **GLP-1** angle — it just hasn't pushed.

## How we measured it (method note)
- **Paid-social intensity → Meta Ad Library active-ad volume.** Ad spend is private (no tool shows real Facebook spend), and clickstream tools under-measure in-app social clicks, so ad *count* is the honest public proxy.
- **Paid-search scale + intent → Semrush** (traffic, top pages, keywords) and Google Ads Transparency Center.
- All figures US, Apr–Jul 2026.
