Key Observations
What the data shows
01

The largest brands in the set are indirect

By estimated traffic, the four biggest brands are all adjacent-symptom brands, not menopause-native ones. The largest direct brand is smaller than all four.

O Positiv 5.8M · Midi 3.6M · Happy Mammoth 2.5M · Alloy 1.7M vs. Bonafide 1.1M
02

No single paid channel leads across the set

The top paid channel differs by brand. Search is most common (4 of 8), with display and social each leading for others.

Search: Estroven, Bonafide, Alloy, Midi · Display: O Positiv, Womaness, Kindra · Social: Happy Mammoth
03

Direct brands' paid share varies widely

Among menopause-native brands, paid share of traffic ranges from 5% to 59%. Two invest heavily; two barely register.

High: Estroven 59%, Bonafide 42% · Low: Womaness 17%, Kindra 5%
On Stripes: traffic is below the reliable reporting threshold of Similarweb and Semrush (est. ~11K/month), so it isn't in the table above. Stripes does run paid social today (visible in Meta Ad Library).
