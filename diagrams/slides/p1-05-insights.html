Key Insights
What the data tells us
01

Outsiders are buying the category's attention

The top paid spenders on menopause intent are adjacent, non-menopause brands - O Positiv, Happy Mammoth, Alloy. Menopause demand is being captured by brands that don't own the space.

Demand exists - it's being intercepted.
02

No incumbent owns paid - the lane is open

Each leader wins on a different engine - O Positiv on display, Happy Mammoth on social, Midi and Alloy on search. There's no single playbook to beat, which makes the channel contestable.

No dominant player to displace.
03

Menopause-native brands have ceded paid

Only Bonafide and Estroven invest meaningfully; the rest lean on SEO, retail and email. The brands most like Stripes are the least visible in performance marketing.

The direct set is barely showing up.
