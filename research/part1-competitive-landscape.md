# Part 1 - Market & Competitive Landscape

**The space.** Stripes is a menopause-wellness brand inside women's health - a life-stage brand (skincare, body, intimate care, supplements, all hormone-free) for women roughly 42-62 in peri/menopause. Our hero product, The Inside Addition, is the daily supplement.

**How I define competitors.** Two yeses: same woman (peri/menopausal) and same need (ease her symptoms). If both, she's a competitor. Then one question sorts it: a menopause brand selling a product like ours is **direct**; a different answer, or a bigger brand catching her through one symptom, is **indirect**.
- **Direct:** Womaness, Kindra, Bonafide, Estroven, Wile, Pause Well-Aging, Valerie.
- **Indirect:** Happy Mammoth (hormones/gut), Midi/Alloy/Evernow (HRT), O Positiv (vaginal probiotics), Nutrafol (hair), Ritual (vitamins), Foria/Good Clean Love/Joylux (intimacy).

**Who leads on paid acquisition** (last 3 months, Apr-Jun 2026; Similarweb, Semrush, Meta Ad Library; estimates, so directional):

| Brand | Type | Visits/mo | Paid social/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| O Positiv | Indirect | ~5.8M | ~234K |
| Midi | Indirect | ~3.6M | ~421K |
| Happy Mammoth | Indirect | ~2.5M | ~632K |
| Alloy | Indirect | ~1.7M | ~119K |
| Bonafide | Direct | ~1.1M | ~46K |
| Estroven / Kindra / Womaness | Direct | ~40-250K | ~0.2-1.8K |

**What the data shows:**
- The top of the paid table isn't menopause brands at all. It's hormones/gut (Happy Mammoth), HRT telehealth (Midi, Alloy) and vaginal probiotics (O Positiv). They buy their growth - organic search is a rounding error for them.
- The actual menopause brands barely run paid. Apart from Bonafide, they lean on SEO and email; their paid social is a few hundred to a couple thousand visits a month.
- For reference, Stripes isn't in the paid game yet (~11K visits/month, ~100 from paid social) and isn't advertising the supplement.

The landscape, in one line: the loudest paid players are adjacent, non-menopause brands, and the menopause-native set competes mostly on organic. That's the backdrop for the launch angle in Part 2.
