Part 2 · Launch Strategy
Creative angle
The positioning
"A daily addition that helps women feel more like themselves again."

Instead of positioning The Inside Addition as another menopause supplement, we position it as a simple daily habit that naturally becomes part of a woman's routine - supporting her from the inside out.

This builds directly on the product name and the brand's existing messaging, rather than inventing a completely new narrative.

Why this angle?
1
Builds on the product name
"The Inside Addition" becomes part of the story - not just the product name.
2
Fits the brand
The messaging feels positive, supportive and optimistic - instead of clinical or fear-based.
3
Stays true to the product
Reflects the existing product messaging:
Daily menopause symptom support
Hormone-free
Supports daily function
Eases stress, brain fog & hot flashes
"Show yourself love from the inside, out."
4
Differentiates from competitors
Most menopause brands focus on individual symptoms. This positions the product as a simple daily addition that supports women throughout their menopause journey.
