Problem

When users first reach the homepage product carousel, it's not immediately obvious which product is right for them. They have to figure it out on their own before deciding where to click, creating unnecessary decision friction.

Hypothesis

Adding lightweight symptom-based product tags will help users quickly identify the product that best matches their primary concern. We expect this to help users make faster, more confident product selections, ultimately improving purchase conversion.

What changes

Simple symptom-based product tags are added above each product name, helping users immediately understand who each product is designed for. Products, pricing and layout remain unchanged. Only product guidance is enhanced.
