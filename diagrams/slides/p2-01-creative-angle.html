Creative angle
The positioning
"Your daily addition. Feel like yourself again."
Rather than another menopause supplement, we position The Inside Addition as a simple daily habit that fits naturally into a woman's routine and supports her from the inside out.
Why this angle?
1
Builds on the product name
"The Inside Addition" becomes part of the story, not just the label.
2
Fits the brand
Positive and supportive, not clinical or fear-based.
3
Matches the product
Daily menopause symptom support
Hormone-free
Supports daily function
Eases stress, brain fog & hot flashes
4
Stands apart from competitors
Most menopause brands focus on a single symptom. We position The Inside Addition as everyday support throughout the menopause journey.
Building on the existing brand message “Show yourself love from the inside, out.”
