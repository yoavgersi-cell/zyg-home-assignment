# Part 2 - Hero Product Launch

## 1. Creative Angle

**The angle: "The hormone-free daily for the symptoms nobody warned you about."**

Open the creative on the everyday reality the product actually helps with - the hot flashes, the brain fog, the low-grade stress of it all - and answer it with one simple daily from Naomi Watts, who got blindsided by menopause herself. She isn't the spokesperson. She's the proof.

**Why this angle.** The category talks to her in two tones: fear ("your hormones are sabotaging you") and medicine ("go get on HRT"). This is neither. It names the symptoms she recognizes, stays warm instead of scary, and leans on the two things Stripes genuinely owns and nobody big can copy - it's hormone-free, and it comes from a woman she trusts who's been through it.

**For whom.** Women roughly 42-62 in perimenopause or menopause who don't want hormones (or can't take them), have already tried something that did nothing, and just want to feel like themselves again without it becoming a medical event.

**Why it wins against what's running.** From Part 1, the loudest paid players each offer her something she isn't actually looking for: a hype hormone pill she doesn't trust (Happy Mammoth), a prescription (Midi, Alloy), probiotics for a different problem (O Positiv), a device (Joylux), or a faceless clinical brand (Bonafide). Ours is the one credible, hormone-free daily with a real face - warm where they're scary, simple where they're clinical, and trusted where the hype brands aren't.
