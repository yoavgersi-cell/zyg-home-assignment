Implications
What this means for Stripes
Three implications follow from the observations. Each is an inference from the data shown, not a measured result.
Observed
Stripes' estimated traffic (~11K/mo) is below every brand in the set; the smallest measured is ~41K.
Implication
The near-term constraint is likely top-of-funnel traffic, not competitive share.
Observed
Across the set, the leading paid channel is split across search, display, and social.
Implication
No single channel is forced by the category - multiple are worth testing.
Observed
Direct-brand paid share ranges 5%–59%; only two of the measured menopause-native brands invest heavily.
Implication
Paid presence from direct competitors is uneven, not saturated.
