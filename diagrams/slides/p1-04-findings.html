What I found
INSIGHT 1

The biggest brands aren't always the closest competitors

The four highest-traffic brands all come from adjacent wellness categories. Bonafide is the largest menopause-focused brand in the comparison.

INSIGHT 2

Paid acquisition looks very different across brands

Some rely heavily on Search. Others invest mostly in Display or Social. There isn't a single winning channel.

INSIGHT 3

Even direct competitors invest differently

Some spend aggressively. Others appear to rely much more on organic growth.
