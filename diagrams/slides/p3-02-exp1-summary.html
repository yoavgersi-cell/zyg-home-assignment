Experiment 01 — Guided Entry Experience
Experiment summary
Hypothesis
Guide visitors to the most relevant product category before asking for an email. By reducing decision friction at the top of the funnel, we expect more users to continue into a relevant shopping journey.
Success metrics
Primary KPI
Product Detail Page View Rate
North star
Supporting business metrics
Purchase Conversion Rate
Revenue per Visitor
Email Signup Rate
Why this experiment first
Targets the highest-volume entry point in the funnel.
Leverages the existing category structure - no new pages.
Requires minimal development effort to ship.
High potential business impact for a low-cost change.
